# Microcement colour options

AI colour-direction studies derived from `../01-doorway-evaluation.png`. The
OpenAI built-in image generator was used in precise-edit mode with official
digital Jotun references. The prompts requested that only the seamless
microcement colour change and that the room, selected products, source view, and
approximately 3000 K lighting remain consistent. Small generated variations in
geometry, materials, and lighting can still occur.

## Beige candidates

- `1276-soft-doorway-v2.webp` — Jotun 1276 Soft, NCS 1507-Y16R. Muted
  golden beige, somewhat grayer than Sans.
- `1519-vanilj-doorway-v2.webp` — Jotun 1519 Vanilj, NCS S1005-Y30R.
  Light classic warm beige with a gentle red undertone.
- `1875-sans-doorway-v2.webp` — Jotun 1875 Sans, NCS 1003-Y21R. Very
  light yellowish warm beige with low chroma.
- `00-neutral-comparison-v2.webp` — unlabelled side-by-side overview.

The earlier PNG studies remain in this folder for provenance; the site uses the
corrected, consistently prompted v2 WebP studies.

Official descriptions and codes:

- [1276 Soft](https://www.jotun.com/se-se/decorative/colours/interior/all-interior-colours/1276-soft-interior)
- [1519 Vanilj](https://www.jotun.com/se-se/decorative/colours/interior/all-interior-colours/1519-vanilla-latte-interior)
- [1875 Sans](https://www.jotun.com/se-se/decorative/colours/interior/all-interior-colours/1875-sense-interior)

## Deliberate contrasts

These are palette-cleansers rather than shortlisted bathroom colours:

- `5504-coastal-blue-doorway.webp` — Jotun 5504 Coastal Blue,
  NCS 5411-B02G. Muted deep greenish blue.
- `8493-green-tea-doorway.webp` — Jotun 8493 Green Tea,
  NCS 3916-G73Y. Muted golden green.
- `20217-muted-coral-doorway.webp` — Jotun 20217 Muted Coral,
  NCS 3234-Y69R. Muted golden coral red.
- `01-palette-cleanser-comparison.webp` — unlabelled side-by-side overview.

The user-supplied fan-deck photos are preserved in `references/`. They identify
the requested colours, while the printed NCS codes were treated as more reliable
than the phone photos' mixed lighting and white balance.

These remain judgement images rather than colour proofs. A sealed physical
microcement sample viewed in the real room under the installed lighting is the
authoritative colour reference. The bold colours are Jotun/NCS reference
directions; Konkral tint availability has not been confirmed.
