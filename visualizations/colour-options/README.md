# Microcement colour options

Controlled doorway-view comparisons derived from
`../01-doorway-evaluation.png`. The OpenAI built-in image generator was used in
precise-edit mode. Only the seamless Konkral Smooth microcement colour was
requested to change; the room, selected products, camera, and 3000 K lighting
were held constant.

## Options

- `1276-soft-doorway.png` — Jotun 1276 Soft, NCS 1507-Y16R. Muted light
  golden beige with a restrained green-grey undertone.
- `1519-vanilj-doorway.png` — Jotun 1519 Vanilj, NCS S1005-Y30R. Light warm
  beige with a gentle red undertone.
- `1875-sans-doorway.png` — Jotun 1875 Sans, NCS 1003-Y21R. Very light,
  subtly warm off-white beige; palest and least red of the three.
- `00-colour-comparison.jpg` — labelled side-by-side overview.

The user-supplied fan-deck photos are preserved in `references/`. They identify
the requested colours, while the printed NCS codes were treated as more reliable
than the phone photos' mixed lighting and white balance.

These remain judgement images rather than colour proofs. A sealed physical
microcement sample viewed in the real room under the installed lighting is the
authoritative colour reference.
