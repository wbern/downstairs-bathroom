## Microcement (mikrocement) — beige, walls & floor

**Key rule:** microcement is a **surface layer, not waterproofing**. Per GVK it must go **on top of an approved tätskikt** (never replace it). The waterproofing must be done by a **BBV-authorised or GVK-certified craftsman** — that certificate is what makes the wet room compliant. *This is the "specific competence" the carpenter referred to.* If microcement goes directly on the tätskikt, the waterproofing supplier must approve it and primer/adhesive should be the same brand (or warranty may be void).

**Rough price:** ~1 600–2 500 kr/m² incl. VAT after ROT (labour + material); both floor and walls count, so a small bathroom often lands from ~30 000 kr. A sample without top-coat looks lighter — judge physical samples in the room's own light.

**Suppliers to contact (Göteborg + beige):**
- Microcement Specialisten — Göteborg, showroom w/ physical samples — https://microcementspecialisten.se/
- GBG Designcement — Göteborg, wide colour range — https://gbgdesigncement.se/
- Microcement Design — Göteborg area, free quote — https://microcementdesign.se/
- Design by Cement — free posted sample chips, 6 colours — https://www.designbycement.com/pages/microcement-badrum
- Badrumsshoppen — ready packs "Sandvit/Beige" & "Gråbeige/Greige" — https://badrumsshoppen.se/microcement

**Beige candidates:** Sandvit/Beige, Gråbeige/Greige (Badrumsshoppen); Topciment Efectto Quartz (sand tones); or a custom tone via Design by Cement free samples.

**Next step:** order free samples from 2–3 suppliers, judge in the room's light, and confirm with Z Bygg who does the tätskikt.
